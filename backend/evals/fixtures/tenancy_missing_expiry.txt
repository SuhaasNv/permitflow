TENANCY AGREEMENT
This tenancy agreement is made between Besar Holdings Pte. Ltd. (the landlord) and Kopi & Kaya Toast House Pte. Ltd. (the tenant) for the premises at 10 Jalan Besar #01-12, Singapore 208787, a shophouse unit of approximately 48 square metres, to be used as a food establishment.
Monthly rent: S$4,800, payable in advance on the first day of each month. Deposit: two months' rent.
The tenant shall keep the premises clean and comply with all licensing requirements.
Signed by the landlord and the tenant in the presence of witnesses.
