(1)BESAR HOLDINGS PTE. LTD. (UEN 201800321W), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 300 Beach Road #22-01, Singapore 199555 (the "Landlord"); and
(2)KOPI & KAYA TOAST HOUSE PTE. LTD. (UEN 202355555E), a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 10 Jalan Besar #01-12, Singapore 208787 (the "Tenant").

Recitals

A.The Landlord is the registered proprietor of the ground floor unit known as 10 Jalan Besar #01-12, Singapore 208787, being a unit within a two-storey shophouse with an internal floor area of approximately 48 square metres (the "Premises").
B.The Tenant wishes to take a tenancy of the Premises for the operation of a food establishment and the Landlord has agreed to grant the tenancy on the terms set out in this Agreement.

Operative provisions

Premises


The Landlord lets and the Tenant takes the Premises described in Schedule 1, together with the right to use the rear service passage and the shared refuse point in common with other occupiers of the building.

Term


The tenancy is for a fixed term of twenty-four (24) months commencing on 1 November 2025 and expiring on 31 October 2027 (the "Term"). The Tenant shall be given vacant possession of the Premises on the commencement date.

Rent


The Tenant shall pay rent of Singapore Dollars Four Thousand Eight Hundred (S$4,800.00) per month, exclusive of goods and services tax, payable monthly in advance on the first day of each calendar month without deduction, by bank transfer to the account notified by the Landlord. Rent for any part of a month shall be apportioned on a daily basis.

Security deposit


On signing this Agreement the Tenant shall pay a security deposit of S$9,600.00 (equivalent to two months' rent), to be held by the Landlord without interest as security for the performance of the Tenant's obligations and refunded, less any lawful deductions, within fourteen (14) days after the Tenant yields up the Premises at the end of the Term.

Permitted use


The Tenant shall use the Premises only as a food establishment for the preparation and sale of kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, kopi, teh and similar light refreshments for consumption on and off the Premises, with seating for not more than twenty-four (24) persons, and for no other purpose without the Landlord's prior written consent.

Licences and approvals


The Tenant shall at its own cost obtain and keep in force all licences, permits and approvals required for the permitted use, including a food establishment licence, and shall comply with all conditions attached to them. The Landlord shall, at the Tenant's request, provide a copy of this Agreement and such written confirmation of the tenancy as any licensing authority reasonably requires.

Fitting out and alterations


The Tenant may install kitchen equipment, an exhaust hood and ducting, a grease trap, partitions, counters and signage in accordance with plans approved in writing by the Landlord (approval not to be unreasonably withheld). Structural alterations are not permitted. The Tenant shall reinstate the Premises at the end of the Term to the condition recorded in the handover inventory, fair wear and tear excepted, unless the Landlord agrees in writing that any fitting may remain.

Repair and cleanliness


The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, including floors, walls, ceilings, doors, windows, sanitary fittings, drains within the Premises and the grease trap, in clean and tenantable repair, and shall engage a licensed pest control contractor at least once every month. The Landlord shall maintain the structure, roof, external walls and main services of the building.

Utilities and outgoings


The Tenant shall pay all charges for electricity, water, gas, telecommunications and refuse collection consumed or used at the Premises and shall transfer the utility accounts into its name within seven (7) days of the commencement date. Property tax is borne by the Landlord.

Insurance


The Tenant shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than S$1,000,000 per occurrence and insurance for its fittings and stock, and shall not do anything that may void the Landlord's fire insurance for the building.

Access


The Landlord may enter the Premises on not less than forty-eight (48) hours' written notice (or without notice in an emergency) to inspect, to carry out repairs or to show the Premises to prospective tenants during the last three (3) months of the Term.

Assignment and subletting


The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, share possession of or otherwise part with possession of the Premises or any part of them.

Default and termination


If the rent is unpaid for fourteen (14) days after falling due, or the Tenant breaches any other term of this Agreement and fails to remedy it within fourteen (14) days of written notice, or the Tenant becomes insolvent, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises and this tenancy shall determine, without prejudice to the Landlord's other rights. Either party may terminate by three (3) months' written notice if the Premises are destroyed or rendered unfit for use by fire or other cause not attributable to the Tenant.

Option to renew


If the Tenant has performed its obligations, the Landlord shall, on the Tenant's written request made not less than three (3) months before the end of the Term, grant a further term of twenty-four (24) months on the same terms (excluding this clause) at a rent to be agreed, failing which at the then prevailing market rent.

Notices


Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post or email to the addresses in Schedule 1. Notices to the Tenant may be addressed to Tan Wei Ling, Director, at weiling.tan@kopikaya.sg.

Stamp duty and costs


The Tenant shall bear the stamp duty on this Agreement and its counterpart. Each party bears its own legal costs.

Governing law


This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the Singapore courts.
