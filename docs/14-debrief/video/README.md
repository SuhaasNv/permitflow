# PermitFlow films

| Folder | Film | Length | What it is |
|--------|------|--------|------------|
| `01-intro/` | `permitflow-intro.mp4` | 3 min 14 s | "Night to Day", the introduction. Act 1 is the old email loop at night in Singapore (Wei Ling's kopi shop, four documents, Rahim comparing pages side by side, a vague "kindly resubmit", hundreds of emails). Then PermitFlow arrives at sunrise, the same journey runs in one animated line (a specific resubmission, compare, approve, the licence), and the AI check helps both sides and never decides. Fully animated, local voices, `subtitles.srt` beside it. The film file itself (262 MB) is kept outside the repository; its poster and subtitles are here. |
| `02-launch/` | `permitflow-launch.mp4` | 70.7 s | Launch video: problem first, then the product. Narrated and captioned. Its `poster.jpg` is also at the top of the repository README. |
| `03-walkthrough/` | `permitflow-walkthrough.mp4` | 4 min 36 s | Narrated walkthrough of both roles, recorded on the development environment. |
| `04-technical/` | `permitflow-technical.mp4` | 5 min 40 s | Roles and the 403s, the status table, the tests, the six-stage AI gate, the fairness sweep, the observability layer, scope, and how it was built. |
| `05-showreel/` | `permitflow-showreel.mp4`, `permitflow-showreel-voiceover.mp4` | 20 s | Motion showreel without and with a voiceover; drawn in code, source and render steps in `source/`. |

All films are 1920 x 1080 at 60 fps. Sources for 01 to 04 are in `notes/pitch/films/` (the intro's rebuild steps are in `notes/pitch/README.md`). Every film describes the application as deployed on `main` (v0.3.0) and is a fictional service built for an engineering assessment.
